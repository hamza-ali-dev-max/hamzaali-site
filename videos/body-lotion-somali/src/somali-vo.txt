Kareemka jirka ma wejiga ayaad marisaa?
Ka taxaddar kareemka culus ama cadarka leh.
Dadka qaarkood wuxuu ku keeni karaa finan ama cuncun.
Dooro kareem wejiga ku habboon, oo aan caraf lahayn, dalooladana aan xirin.
Kareem kasta oo jirka loogu talagalay ma xuma; waxa muhiimka ahi waa inuu maqaarkaaga ku habboon yahay.
Maqaarkaaga daryeel, summaddana akhri!
