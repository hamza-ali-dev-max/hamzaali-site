What it does

It does the work of a front desk, on every call.

  • Answers every call

    Day and night. Weekends and public holidays too.

  • Books appointments

    Straight into your calendar, while the caller is on the line.

  • Answers questions

    Hours, prices, services and directions.

  • Takes messages

    Name, number and what the call was about.

  • Sends you the details

    On WhatsApp and email, after every call.

  • Speaks three languages

    English, Afrikaans and isiXhosa. It switches in the middle of a call when the caller does.
