    It knows when to hand over

    It books the simple calls and passes the rest to you, with the details already on your WhatsApp.

    • The caller asks for a person

      "Of course. I'm putting you through to Naledi now."

    • It's urgent

      "That sounds urgent. Can I get your name and number in case the line drops? Putting you through to Thabo now."

    • It doesn't know the answer

      "I don't have that information, but I'll ask Dr Naidoo to get back to you today."

    What it won't do

    • Pretend to be a person
    • Give medical, legal or technical advice
    • Ask for card numbers or ID numbers
    • Promise anything outside your rules
