## The headline findings

1. **Federal IT has no degree gate.** The IT group standard requires a TWO-YEAR post-secondary program OR "an acceptable combination of education, training and experience" at manager discretion. Hamza's 2 completed CS years may be assessed as meeting it directly. Order transcripts now.
2. **PR status is equal to citizen for core federal hiring** (PSEA amendment, in force). Exception: CRA ranks PRs behind citizens.
3. **GC Digital Talent (talent.canada.ca) has live IT-02 pools** in 2026 and explicitly values skills over formal education. IT-01 starts ~$69K (~$35/hr): even entry level beats the $28/hr floor. Federal timeline is 6-12 months, so this is the background track, not income now.
4. **BC Public Service entry rung is ISL 18R** (Service Desk / Business Support Analyst), ~$64-73K, with a published equivalency ladder ("or an equivalent combination of education and experience"). ISL 11/13 no longer exist. Create the RMS profile; postings close 11pm Pacific; eligibility lists last 6-12 months so a #2 finish still converts.
5. **ICBC IT Service Desk Analyst (North Vancouver) is live** (posted May 2026, $67,048-$72,399, hybrid): lowest formal barrier in the crown lane, wants diploma/cert + 2 yrs IT service delivery. Insurance employer: Hamza's judgment call (conventional insurance is not on the exclusion list).
6. **The Dynamics 365 Business Central ecosystem is the killer lane.** Catapult ERP (Vancouver) has an explicitly entry-level D365 Finance Consultant opening (apply: careers@catapulterp.com). Encore Business Solutions' BC support role explicitly counts end-user/internal-support experience. Endeavour Solutions recruits BC/NAV consultants Canada-wide (HR@endeavoursolutions.ca). 2026 talent shortage: partners turning down projects for lack of staff.
7. **Cert plan: MB-800 first** (~CAD $220, free Microsoft Learn prep, 6-10 weeks, no prerequisites, no degree needed), **PL-300 (Power BI) second**. Skip the CompTIA trinity (leads to $24/hr helpdesk, a downgrade), skip AZ-900/AWS CCP (never posting requirements), do NOT start PL-200 (retires Aug 31, 2026).

- Public-sector service desks pay $30-40/hr entry with no degree gate: TransLink Service Desk Analyst ($5,818-$6,979/mo), PHSA Support Analyst (~$34/hr), Fraser Health (Surrey, home turf).
- Private tier-1 helpdesk in Vancouver averages $21.60-$24/hr: below floor. MSPs (F12.net Surrey, Microserve Burnaby) are wage-sacrifice-for-velocity plays only if nothing else lands in 8-10 weeks; "two years at an MSP = five years elsewhere."

- Skip Geek Squad ($18-21/hr BC, weak resume signal).
